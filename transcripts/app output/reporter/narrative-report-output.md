# Heart Walk Interview Research

**Summary of Findings and Recommendations**

---

## Executive Summary

• In-depth interviews with Heart Walk staff, including Katie Belusa, Lydia Hazel, Kenz Geschke, Lauren Verrill, and Caleb Bone, reveal critical insights into organizational dynamics and operational challenges.
• The Executive Leadership Team (ELT) is identified as the central hub for coordination, yet recruitment struggles persist due to high turnover and limited candidate pools.
• Young leaders report significant trust barriers, citing a lack of mentorship and perceived exclusion from decision-making processes.
• Resource and communication gaps are evident, with staff highlighting inconsistent access to standardized ELT resources and ineffective use of Salesforce tools.
• A key tension arises between the need for cross-market collaboration and the competitive nature of regional fundraising efforts, which can hinder unified strategies.
• To address these challenges, the report recommends enhancing onboarding with tailored training modules and leveraging volunteer connectors to strengthen community ties.

• Meetings facilitate recruitment and leadership development, introducing team members and outlining goals.

> **Quote Bubble — Lauren Verrill**

## Challenges in ELT Recruitment

> **Quote Bubble — Lauren Verrill**
> "We have a running list of prospects, so it's not something we start from scratch every year."

• Securing a chair involves a $125,000 corporate commitment, often over three years.
• 75% of the time, the next year's chair is confirmed, but going two years out is only 10%.
• Finding enthusiastic and committed ELT members remains a significant challenge.

> **Quote Bubble — Caleb Bone**
> "The people we really want have either sat on it or don't have capacity."

## Resource and Communication Gaps

> **Quote Bubble — Lauren Verrill**
> "I noticed there's an ELT chair job aid, but no ELT member job aid. So, I created one myself."

• Staff create their own ELT materials because national resources don't meet market needs.

> **Quote Bubble — Katie Belusa**

## Navigating Political and Economic Hurdles

> **Quote Bubble — Katie Belusa**
