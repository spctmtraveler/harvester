## --- Page 39 ---

## SWOT Snapshot

**Strengths**

* Strong brand
* Well-run organization
* Powerful mission
* Strong networks

**Weaknesses**

* Loss of old staff
* New/inexperienced staff

**Opportunities**

* Leverage mission for stronger why
* Simplify best practices
* Deepen existing relationships

**Threats**

* Older volunteers tapped out
* Understaffed markets
* Scrambling, short-term focus

## --- Page 40 ---

## Mapping the Process (Simplified)

**Chair Pipeline**

* Leadership recruitment roundtable
* Identify prospects
* Identify dream team
* Chair ask
* Chair orientation
* Recruit successor

**ELT Process**

* Recruit ELT
* Staff orientation & impact plan
* Working meetings
* 4 ELT meetings annually

**Best Practices**

* Secure 3 years of chairs
* 15+ ELT members
* Early recruitment drives revenue

# Behavioral Levers

---

## --- Page 46 ---

## Levers of Change

### 1. Clarity & Structure

* Simplify staff priorities
* Highly structured Chair/ELT time

### 2. Stronger Emotional WHY

* Less transactional
* Focus on their why
* Make mission salient

### 3. Leverage Existing Relationships

* Maximize exec network
* Tap into family identity

---

## --- Page 47 ---

## No Clarity = Hard

## Clarity & Structure = Easy

Provide drafted emails, structured outreach, objection handling.

---

## --- Page 48 ---

## Focus the Week

Instead of a long to-do list:

**Must Do:**
Email top 3 Chair prospects for coffee (with volunteer).

## --- Page 49 ---

## Clarity & Structure – What It Means

**For Staff**

* Simplify priorities
* Align recruitment with evaluations
* Do fewer things well

**For Chairs/ELT**

* White glove service
* Structured time
* No homework
