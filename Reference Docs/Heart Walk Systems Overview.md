# Heart Walk — System Overview (AI Context Document)

A concise description of the whole Heart Walk system. It is intended as neutral context for AI parsing and analysis. Should be included in every AP Prompt for context.

---

## 1. Purpose of Heart Walk

Heart Walk is a market-based fundraising and engagement campaign operated by the American Heart Association (AHA).
Its primary goals are:

* Generate corporate sponsorship and philanthropic revenue
* Engage companies and community leaders
* Activate employee participation and fundraising
* Build long-term leadership pipelines in each market

---

## 2. Organizational Structure

### National Level (AHA)

* Sets overall strategy, goals, and brand standards
* Develops campaign resources and materials
* Provides reporting systems and tools
* Offers consulting and regional oversight

### Market Level (Local AHA Office)

* Executes Heart Walk within a defined geographic area
* Recruits and manages volunteer leadership
* Manages corporate relationships and fundraising pipeline
* Coordinates campaign meetings and events

---

## 3. Leadership Structure

Heart Walk operates through two leadership layers.

### A. Internal AHA Leadership (Paid Staff)

Roles typically include:

* Executive Director (ED)
* Development Director (DD)
* Senior Development Director
* Senior Vice President (SVP)
* Director

Primary responsibilities:

* Recruit volunteer leadership
* Coach and support outreach efforts
* Prepare materials and meeting agendas
* Track revenue pipeline and commitments
* Coordinate internal reporting and data systems

### B. External Volunteer Leadership

Roles typically include:

* ELT Chair
* ELT (Executive Leadership Team) Members
* Market Board Members
* Corporate Executives
* Team Captains

Primary responsibilities:

* Open access to corporate decision-makers
* Make sponsorship and gift asks
* Recruit additional leaders
* Mobilize company participation
* Advocate for the campaign within their networks

---

## 4. Core Campaign Components

### A. Leadership Recruitment

* Identify and recruit an ELT Chair
* Recruit ELT members (typically corporate executives)
* Align volunteer leaders with campaign goals and expectations

### B. Corporate Engagement

* Identify corporate prospects
* Conduct sponsorship and fundraising asks
* Recruit companies to form employee fundraising teams

### C. Meetings & Cadence

Common meeting types include:

* ELT meetings (multi-session cadence during campaign)
* Strategy or GAP meetings (pipeline vs target review)
* Internal coordination meetings among staff

Campaign arc typically includes:

* Leadership recruitment phase
* Corporate prospecting and sponsorship phase
* Participation and fundraising activation phase
* Stewardship and recognition phase

## 6. Relationship Model

Heart Walk functions as a relationship-based system.

* Internal staff recruit and support volunteer leaders.
* Volunteer leaders conduct outreach to companies and peers.
* Companies sponsor and mobilize employees.
* Revenue and participation are tracked through internal systems.

Relationship ownership is coordinated within the market to manage corporate access.

Core recurring actions include:

* Capture commitments
* Track pipeline status
